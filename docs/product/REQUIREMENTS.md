# Product requirements

The master specification governs scope and acceptance. ComplyLet serves independent English letting agencies managing 50–500 properties, initially in London and Greater Manchester. It must show applicable obligations, responsible parties, deadlines, evidence and historical verification. Unknown applicability remains explicit. The Compliance Passport is a selectively shareable evidence record.

Core journeys cover agency onboarding, portfolio import, regulatory assessment, evidence review, statutory communication records, tasks, readiness and evidence export. Regulatory content requires official sources, versioned deterministic rules and separate author/reviewer approval. Extracted document data requires human validation. Commercial scope includes property-based subscriptions and later restricted landlord access.

Use British English and target WCAG 2.2 AA. Treat performance and recovery figures in the master specification as verification targets. Council selection, production identity provider and legal and privacy reviews remain open.
